# Prior-Art and Novelty Boundary

**Status:** unverified research boundary  
**Purpose:** prevent SAI EpochCast and Open Calculator from claiming novelty more broadly than available evidence supports

---

## 1. Current claim

Open Calculator presents a distinctive synthesis:

- a single globally shared calculator expression;
- contested ordered input;
- a paid atomic `=` operation that creates card ownership;
- replayable event and state commitments;
- GPT-5.6 canonization that is separated from economic authority;
- a CCL evidence layer;
- SAI EpochCast, which separates active authoritative interaction from spectator/history coherence.

The project may confidently claim that this is its published architecture and product design.

The project does **not** currently claim:

- invention of event sourcing;
- invention of sequencing;
- invention of microbatching;
- invention of Merkle trees;
- invention of transactional outbox;
- invention of WebSocket relay trees;
- invention of append-only streams;
- invention of offset recovery;
- invention of state hashes;
- invention of deterministic replay;
- invention of WebAssembly evaluation;
- invention of AI-generated metadata;
- academic or patent priority for the combined protocol.

---

## 2. Potential contribution unit

The defensible research unit, if evidence supports it, is not an isolated mechanism. It is the specific combination of:

1. active-only epoch folding for a contested globally shared state;
2. a deterministic manifest and state transition commitment;
3. a special Strong Path that defines an atomic ownership cut;
4. active relay coherence separated from spectator stream coherence;
5. state-hash lane handoff and offset recovery;
6. replayable card birth tied to economic settlement;
7. public epistemic tracking through CCL.

Even this combined contribution requires comparison with prior systems and measurement against simpler baselines.

---

## 3. Prior-art categories to investigate

A formal search should cover at least:

- replicated state machines and total-order broadcast;
- multiplayer authoritative servers;
- chat and collaborative editing systems;
- event sourcing and CQRS;
- stream-processing epochs and watermarks;
- microbatch processing;
- multiplayer lockstep and rollback networking;
- Merkle logs and transparency systems;
- transactional outbox and change-data capture;
- pub/sub relay trees and content distribution;
- spectator modes in games and live events;
- append-only logs with offset replay;
- state channels and commit-reveal systems;
- auction and exchange matching engines;
- deterministic simulation and replay witnesses;
- verifiable computation and execution proofs;
- forkable event histories;
- AI-assisted metadata and lore generation.

---

## 4. Comparison questions

For each relevant system or paper, record:

```text
What is authoritative?
How is order defined?
Are commands folded into epochs?
Can an epoch be replayed deterministically?
Is there an explicit economic or ownership cut?
How are active users served?
How are spectators served?
Does the authority directly fan out to readers?
Is there an append-only public history?
Can a client switch lanes with a state commitment?
Are forks supported?
What does a Merkle or hash commitment prove?
What fairness model is claimed?
What failure recovery is demonstrated?
What workload and scale are measured?
```

---

## 5. Required evidence before a novelty claim

A novelty statement should not be promoted until:

- the prior-art search method is documented;
- high-relevance systems are compared directly;
- overlapping terminology is identified;
- known components are credited;
- the proposed contribution is stated narrowly;
- at least one implementation result demonstrates a benefit or new capability;
- external technical review has had an opportunity to challenge the boundary.

Until then, CCL status remains `unverified`.

---

## 6. Safe language

Appropriate:

> SAI EpochCast is an experimental protocol architecture synthesized for Open Calculator.

> It combines known distributed-systems techniques around a spectator-heavy, ownership-creating global state machine.

> The project publishes the combination, semantics, weaknesses, and validation plan for review.

> Its performance and novelty remain unverified until benchmark and prior-art work are complete.

Inappropriate before evidence:

> EpochCast is the world’s first global communication protocol of its kind.

> EpochCast solves global scale.

> EpochCast provides zero-cost broadcasting.

> Merkle roots prove the system is correct.

> The protocol can support a stated audience size without a reproducible benchmark.

---

## 7. Model provenance and authorship

The project publicly records that SAI, operating through GPT-5.6 under human direction, synthesized EpochCast’s named architecture and its normalized research specification.

This provenance is meaningful but does not settle academic authorship, legal inventorship, or patentability. Those questions require separate expert analysis and cannot be inferred from a repository contribution statement.

---

## 8. Research outcome possibilities

The prior-art and benchmark program can produce several honest outcomes.

### Outcome A — distinct and beneficial synthesis

Relevant prior systems do not combine the same semantics, and EpochCast demonstrates measurable advantage for the target workload.

### Outcome B — useful application architecture, limited novelty

The combination is a strong engineering design for Open Calculator but closely follows established patterns.

### Outcome C — no advantage over simpler baseline

EpochCast’s extra complexity fails to improve cost, latency, correctness, or inspectability sufficiently.

### Outcome D — partial contribution

One component, such as the Equal Cut Contract or dual-lane replay handoff, proves useful while the larger protocol is simplified.

All four outcomes are valuable CCL evidence. The repository should not be organized so that only Outcome A can be reported as success.
